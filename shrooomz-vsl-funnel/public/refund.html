Refund Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

90-day money-back guarantee

If it's not for you, email us within 90 days of your order and we'll refund you.

How it works

  • Email help@secretshrooomz.com with "Refund" in the subject line.
  • Tell us the email you ordered with, and your order number if you have it.
  • You don't need to explain yourself, but we always like to hear what happened.

What happens next

We confirm by email, then the refund goes back to the card you paid with. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

Do I send the product back?

No. Keep it. No forms, no return shipping, no questions.

Upsells and extra items

Anything you added later is covered by the same guarantee, for the same 90 days from the date of that charge.

Outside the window

If you're a little past 90 days, email us anyway. We'd rather keep you than win an argument.
