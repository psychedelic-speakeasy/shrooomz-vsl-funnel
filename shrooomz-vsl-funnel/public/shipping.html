Shipping Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Where we ship

Within the United States.

How long it takes

  • Orders are packed within 1 business day.
  • Delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days after that.
  • Weekends and holidays can add a day or two.

Cost

Free shipping on orders over $150. Below that, shipping is a flat $9.99.

Discreet packaging

Your order ships in a plain box. Nothing on the outside says what is inside.

Tracking

You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

Wrong or missing address

Email help@secretshrooomz.com as soon as you notice. If the order hasn't shipped, we can usually change it. If it has, we'll tell you what your options are.

Lost or delayed

If tracking hasn't moved in 5 business days, email us and we'll chase it with the carrier.
