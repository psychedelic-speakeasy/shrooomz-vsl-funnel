Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Shrooomz LLC ("we", "us") runs secretshrooomz.com. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you opt in to watch the video.
  • Your name, email and shipping address, when you place an order.
  • What you ordered, and when.
  • Your card details are handled by Stripe. We never see the card number.

How we use it

To send you the video and follow-up emails, to ship your order, to answer your questions, and to see which ads are working.

Cookies and tracking

We use the Meta pixel and Cortana to measure visits and purchases. These set cookies in your browser. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Who we share with

  • Stripe, to take payment.
  • Our email platform, to send your emails.
  • Our shipping partner, to deliver your order.

We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to run the business, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom. Ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, by emailing help@secretshrooomz.com.
